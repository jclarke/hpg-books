Your books, on your machine, encrypted.

HPG Books imports your bank and card transactions, sorts them with rules you control, and gives you a P&L for the business and net worth, budgets, and bills for the household. In one encrypted database, with nothing sent anywhere.

What it does

Import

Transactions from the Finance connector, Capital One and Apple Card CSVs, store-card statement PDFs, and Monarch exports. Re-imports never duplicate, and pending rows are matched to their posted versions.

Classify

Priority-ordered rules, "create a rule from this", a review inbox with suggestions, bulk edits with undo, and transfer matching. Your manual decisions are never overwritten.

Report

P&L by month or by business, cash flow, expenses by vendor, owner draws, a Schedule C-style summary, and a reconciliation. Export to CSV, XLSX, and PDF.

Track payments

Every card and loan with its minimum payment, due date, APR, and autopay, plus what's due in the next 30 days and anything overdue.

Several businesses

Run more than one business in a single ledger, each with its own label and colour, and see them side by side.

Stay current

hpgbooks update check tells you when there's a new version; updates only fast-forward and never touch your data or config.

Business mode and personal mode

One database, two sets of books. A Business | Personal toggle in the header switches between them, and every account belongs to one side.

Business

The business ledger

  • Dashboard with revenue, expenses, and net income against the prior period
  • Profit & loss by month or by business; click a figure to see its transactions
  • Review inbox, largest first, with "Accept + rule"
  • Owner draws kept below the line, never counted as an expense
  • Expenses by vendor and a year-end Schedule C-style summary
Personal

The household

  • Net worth history from balances plus activity
  • Spending by category or merchant, and cash flow
  • Budgets with rollover, pace, and 80% alerts
  • Recurring charges and subscriptions, with price changes flagged
  • Bills, goals, debt payoff, and a monthly summary

Private by design

Encrypted database

Everything lives in one SQLCipher-encrypted file. The key sits in a mode-600 file on your computer and is never printed or committed.

Local only

The web app listens on 127.0.0.1 and nothing else. For your phone or laptop, optional Tailscale access adds a host allowlist and a passphrase.

No third parties

The app makes no third-party requests and no network calls for ledger data. Transactions arrive as files saved on your computer, and stay there.

Get it with Grok Bot

Start from the Grok Bot template

The template sets up a Grok Bot with the books app, its skills, and the routines below. Grok Bot installs everything on its own cloud computer and asks you a few questions; nothing runs on your laptop.

Get the Grok Bot template

Or set it up from a plain chat:

  1. Ask Grok Bot to install it

    Paste this into a chat:

    Install the books app from https://github.com/jclarke/hpg-books on your computer and set it up for me.

    It clones the repository, creates the database key without ever showing it, writes your config, creates the database, starts the web app, and runs the tests.

  2. Answer a few questions

    Your company name, your timezone, whether you want business books, personal, or both, your businesses and categories, and which accounts to bring in from the Finance connector you've linked in Grok Bot.

  3. Just ask

    • "Sync my books"
    • "Show me my P&L for September"
    • "What's uncategorized?"
    • "When are my cards due?"
    • "How's my spending this month?"
    • "Export a PDF report"
    • "Check for updates"

    Grok Bot can also run the syncs, the health check, and the update check on a schedule. See the routines.

  4. Open the web app

    On the computer where the books run, go to http://127.0.0.1:8765. To open it from elsewhere, ask Grok Bot to set up Tailscale for your books.

Grok Bot routines to keep it updated

The template schedules these for you; set them up by hand from a plain chat if you didn't start from it. Times are suggestions in your own timezone, spread out so the jobs don't overlap.

Daily business books sync

Weekdays, about 7:20am

Pulls recent transactions for your business accounts through the Finance connector, imports them (re-imports never duplicate), refreshes balances, applies your rules, and tells you what still needs a decision.

Daily personal books sync

Every day, about 7:43am

The same for your personal accounts: new transactions, balances, and net worth, plus anything new in the review queue.

Daily payment due dates update

Every day, about 8:12am

Refreshes minimum payments, due dates, and APRs for your cards and loans, so the Bills page and "When are my cards due?" stay current.

Weekly Monarch pull

Sundays

For accounts the Finance connector doesn't cover, pulls a Monarch export and imports only the rows the ledger is missing, then updates those accounts' balances.

Books app health check

Every 30 minutes

Checks that the web server answers. If it is down it restarts the server, and Tailscale too when remote access is set up. Quiet when everything is fine.

Update checker

Every day

Runs bin/hpgbooks update check and only messages you when there is a new version. It never applies an update on its own.

The template also ships three skills: books-getting-started (first install and setup), daily-books-sync (the business sync), and daily-personal-books-sync (the personal sync).
